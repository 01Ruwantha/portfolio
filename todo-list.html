Overview

Developed and published a productivity-focused mobile application, Todo List, using Flutter and Provider. The app helps users manage daily tasks with a clean, intuitive interface and essential features like adding, deleting, and marking tasks as completed.

Key Highlights & Engineering Decisions

• Simplicity First: Designed with zero bloat, allowing users to capture thoughts and actionable to-dos instantly.
• Flutter & Dart: Single codebase engineered for fluid 60fps performance and responsive scaling across mobile phones and tablets.
• State Management via Provider: Decoupled UI and business logic for predictable and bug-free task state updates.
• 100% Privacy Commitment: Operates entirely offline on the client device. Absolutely no user data is collected, tracked, or transmitted.
• Production Release: Packaged, signed, and published live on the Google Play Store through Google Play Console workflows.
