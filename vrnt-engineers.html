Overview

VRNT Engineers is a specialized mobile application engineered for construction contractors, site engineers, and project managers to streamline estimation, invoicing, and quotation workflows.

Key Features

• Instant PDF Invoices: Generates formatted PDFs with itemized breakdown and one-tap WhatsApp / Email sharing.
• Custom Categories: Create tailored categories for materials, labor, machinery, masonry, roofing, and plumbing.
• Bank & Business Details: Save account, branch, and company branding settings.
• Offline Reliability: Works seamlessly on active job sites without requiring internet connectivity.
• Calendar Task Planning: Keep track of project milestones and invoice due dates.
