🐱 The Ultimate Hunting Training Game for Cats

Cat Tap Hunt is a thrilling interactive hunting simulation designed to awaken cats' natural predatory instincts. Built and optimized for high-touch responsiveness, it provides continuous stimulation for kittens and indoor cats.

✨ Key Features

• Automated Video Capture: Captures video highlights while your cat plays, saved directly to local storage.
• 11 Unique Prey Species: Bird, Squirrel, Frog, Porcupine, Mouse, Fox, Praying Mantis, Rabbit, Mole, Salamander, and Cockroach.
• Customizable Physics: Adjust prey speed (slow to lightning fast), size (150px–320px), and number of prey simultaneously.
• Zero Distraction: Strictly no ads during active gameplay to ensure an uninterrupted experience.
